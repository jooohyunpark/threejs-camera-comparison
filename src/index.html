Perspective vs Orthographic

Mimics the human eye. The frustum is a pyramid, so objects shrink with distance and parallel lines converge toward a vanishing point. Scrolling moves the camera closer.

Lower the field of view: the camera backs away to keep the same framing, and the image flattens toward orthographic.

Projects along parallel lines. The frustum is a box, so on-screen size doesn't depend on distance and parallel lines stay parallel. Scrolling changes the box size (zoom), not the camera position.

Common in CAD, isometric and 2D games, UI overlays and directional light shadow maps.

Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
